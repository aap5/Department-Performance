Finding
New hires are the single biggest attrition exposure. Employees under 1 year of tenure separate at 47.8%, nearly 3x the year-2-4 rate — most of that gap closes by year 4, so the first 12 months of onboarding and management quality is where a retention program pays off fastest.

Avg. Salary by Gender, within Job Level

Finding
A modest but consistent pay gap favors men at every level, ranging 1.4%–5.8% and widest at Associate and Lead. It's not extreme, but it's systemic rather than random — worth a level-by-level compensation audit.
